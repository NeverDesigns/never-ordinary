# Never Ordinary — Open Source Higgsfield submission (README draft)

**Tagline:** Your photos. Impossible adventures.

## What it is

Never Ordinary is a ComfyUI app built on the open-weights MiniMax H3 video model. It does the thing Higgsfield made famous — put a real person into an impossible video — for the people who actually matter to you: your friends, your kids, yourself.

Give it two photos of a person and pick an adventure. Your friend's snapshot becomes him surfing a monster wave, still unmistakably him. A kid on a backyard swing is suddenly swinging above the clouds in a fantasy landscape. A driveway photo becomes a stadium celebration. The emotional hook needs no explanation: *"That's actually me, doing something impossible."*

## Why it can win

- **Made to be shared.** Every output is a gift, not a demo — parents send the adventure clips to grandparents; friends send each other stunt videos. Sharing is the use case, not a marketing layer.
- **Open weights only.** Everything runs locally: MiniMax H3 reference-to-video (pruned INT8 build), the Lemon3-VL text encoder, and H3's own video/audio VAEs. No partner nodes, no closed APIs, no cloud. Developed and rendered on a single consumer GPU (RTX 5070 Ti, 16 GB).
- **Sound included.** H3 generates audio with the video — waves, crowds, laughter — in the same pass.
- **Presets, not promptcraft.** Three one-click Adventure Presets at launch (Monster Wave, Stadium Celebration, Fantasy Vista) plus a custom mode, each written to protect the face: visible, front-on, gently moving camera.
- **Honest identity craft.** The app shows its identity quality instead of guaranteeing it: a sharp close-up anchors every generation, descriptors are matched to that close-up, and scenes are staged for face visibility. Short, excellent clips over long, inconsistent ones.

## How it works

1. **Reference 1 — the close-up.** A sharp, front-facing photo of the person's face. This is the identity anchor.
2. **Reference 2 — the ordinary photo.** Outfit and context.
3. **The identity block.** Every prompt opens with a fixed identity instruction whose descriptors (hair, glasses, facial hair) are matched to the close-up itself.
4. **Locked quality defaults.** Reference size `max`; one scene per ~5-second clip (124 frames at 24 fps).
5. **The scene.** A preset (or custom) scene written to keep the hero's face visible and fully in frame.

## Install & use

1. Install ComfyUI (recent build with the native MiniMax H3 nodes).
2. Place the open-weights models in `models/`:
   - `diffusion_models/minimax_h3_ref2va_pruned_int8_convrot.safetensors`
   - `text_encoders/qwen3vl_32b_minimax_h3_int8_convrot.safetensors`
   - `vae/minimax_h3_video_vae_fp16.safetensors`
   - `vae/minimax_h3_audio_vae_fp32.safetensors`
   (MiniMax H3 open weights, via Hugging Face.)
3. Open the workflow, replace the two Load Image nodes with your person's close-up and photo, choose a preset from `adventure-presets.md`, adjust the identity descriptors to match the close-up, and queue.

Files in this package: the app workflow (UI + API JSON), `adventure-presets.md`, demo video, this README.

## Demo provenance

Every clip in the demo video was generated by this exact workflow on a local RTX 5070 Ti — real photos of real people (the creator's friends and family), transformed with the presets above. No cloud generation, no partner nodes, no post-hoc face swaps.
