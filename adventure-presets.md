# Never Ordinary — Adventure Presets

Every prompt has two parts. **Part 1 (identity block) never changes structure** — only the person's descriptors change, and they must match the close-up photo: hair, glasses, facial hair, age. **Part 2 (the scene)** is the preset. Paste both into the prompt box, in order.

Identity block template:

> \<Picture 1> is a close-up photo of the person's face and \<Picture 2> shows the SAME person — use them as the exact visual reference for the hero's identity and keep their face identical to them in every single frame: same face, [hair], [glasses / none], [facial hair]. Do not change, beautify or swap their face at any point.

House rules (learned across dozens of real runs):
- Sharp, front-facing close-up as reference 1 — it does the heavy lifting. An ordinary second photo gives outfit/context.
- Scenes are written to keep the hero's face visible, fully in frame, and mostly toward the camera, with limited camera motion. Face size, angle, and occlusion decide fidelity as much as the reference does.
- One scene per clip (~5 seconds, 124 frames). Short and excellent beats long and inconsistent.

## Launch presets (v1)

### 1. Monster Wave — the action scene
Photorealistic action video, bright golden-hour ocean: the same person is surfing a giant turquoise wave on a surfboard, riding down the face of the wave toward the camera with arms out for balance, knees bent, whooping with joy, their face clearly visible and turned toward the camera the whole time, white spray flying behind them, sparkling water, epic scale, pure delight. Audio: crashing waves, wind, joyful shouting and laughter.
*Settings: 896×672, 124 frames.*

### 2. Stadium Celebration — the triumph scene
Photorealistic video in a packed championship stadium at night: the same person celebrates at the center of the field as confetti cannons erupt around them and the crowd roars; they raise both arms in victory and grin straight at the camera, face clearly visible the whole time, stadium floodlights blazing, gold confetti raining down, pure triumph. Audio: a roaring stadium crowd, confetti cannons, swelling celebration music.
*Settings: 896×672, 124 frames. Generic arena — no league branding.*

### 3. Fantasy Vista — the wonder scene (family favorite)
Photorealistic video at golden sunrise: the same person stands on a majestic grassy cliff edge high above a sea of clouds, arms spread wide, grinning at the camera with pure wonder, face clearly visible the whole time, eagles gliding below, floating green islands in the far distance, an epic fantasy landscape. Audio: soft wind, distant eagle cries, joyful laughter.
*Settings: 896×672, 124 frames. For kids: same scene, swing set on the cliff top, swinging out over the clouds and back — playful fantasy, never peril.*

## Custom
Write any scene after the identity block. One location, one action, face toward camera, gentle camera motion, ~5 seconds. End with an "Audio:" line — MiniMax H3 generates the sound with the picture.
